The Stock Pitch
Challenge.

A real research budget. An industry mentor. A stage to defend an idea in front of people who'll actually push back. Built for students who've never had a way into a room like this.

Overview

Pick a stock. Build the case.
Defend it live.

Teams research a public company, build a full investment thesis, and pitch it to a panel of working analysts and portfolio managers. It's not a classroom exercise — the questions are the same ones a real pitch would face, and the panel is under no obligation to be gentle about it.

How it works

Four stages.

01

Form a team

Small teams — no finance background required. Some of the strongest teams we've seen came in with more curiosity than credentials.

02

Research & build the thesis

Each team gets a research budget and access to a mentor from the industry to sanity-check their thinking as the thesis develops.

03

Pitch live

Teams present their case to a panel of analysts and portfolio managers, then take real questions — the kind meant to find the weak point in an argument, not just to be polite.

04

Win funding & placements

Top teams receive prize funding, and are fast-tracked toward internship and job conversations with our partner firms.

Who it's for

You don't need a finance
degree to enter.

The Challenge is built for people who've never been handed a way in — students from any major, at any school, with any level of prior exposure to markets. What matters is whether you can build an argument and defend it, not where you learned to do it.
