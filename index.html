The Mission

Capital should move toward
potential, not just proximity.

Financial literacy is one of the clearest predictors of long-term stability — and one of the most unevenly distributed. We build the education, the competition, and the connections that a legacy network would otherwise provide, and we give them away for free.

Programs

Four ways in.

01

Learn

AI-guided courses that adapt to what a learner already knows — budgeting, credit, investing, and markets — for people with no formal finance background, free and self-paced.

02

Compete

The Stock Pitch Challenge gives students a real research budget, an industry mentor, and a stage — top teams pitch live to analysts and portfolio managers for funding and internship placements.

03

Connect

We partner with banks, asset managers, and fintechs to build a direct line from our courses into internships and entry-level roles — with a focus on candidates who rarely get a warm introduction.

04

Read & Listen

Original blogs, plain-language economic research, and a podcast interviewing the people shaping markets — written and produced for readers who are new to finance, not just fluent in it.
